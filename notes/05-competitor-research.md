# Competitor research (Round 6, 2026-11-06)

Sam asked: "find website competitor example online, and try to copy what other page or other section need to add."
**Rule I used: take ideas and structure only. No text, photos or designs were copied.** Everything on the Pedalwise pages was drawn from scratch with the client's own facts.

## Sources looked at (structure only)
| Source | What it is | Read how |
|---|---|---|
| campusbikeandskate.ucsd.edu | A campus bike and skate shop with an online store | Page structure read through a text fetch (menu, collection page, footer). I did not view it visually. |
| Spoke Cycles (Framer template, framer.com/marketplace/templates/spoke-cycles) | A bike shop website template | Section list from the template description. |
| Bikingly (Shopify theme, monsterone.com) | A bicycle store theme | Feature list from the theme page. |

Limit: these are descriptions read as text, so they show which sections exist, not how well they work.

## Ideas adopted (and where)
| Idea | Where it is on the Pedalwise pages |
|---|---|
| Header with a cart link | "Cart" button with a count badge in every menu |
| Info strip (hours, place, contact) near the top | Home, under the hero (client facts only) |
| Category tiles | Home "Shop by category", About "From our shop" |
| Featured products row | Home, Services (accessories), Prices (merchandise) |
| Collection page with filter chips and a sort control | Shop page (chips are drawn, not working) |
| Breadcrumb, size choice, quantity stepper, details accordion, related products | Product page |
| Cart with order summary and "continue shopping" | Cart page |
| Checkout split into contact, delivery, payment plus a summary | Checkout page (empty fields only) |
| Order confirmation page | Confirmation page |
| Newsletter block | Home, About, Contact (design only, no emails collected) |

## Ideas skipped on purpose
- **Reviews, star ratings, "trusted by" logos, brand strip:** I would have to invent them.
- **Part-exchange, cycle-to-work, service booking form, group rides, blog:** not in the client's brief. Blog is a separate phase-2 estimate (notes/03).
- **Payment method logos and security badges:** invented claims.
- **Multiple currencies, accounts, wishlist, quick view:** too much for a first shop design.

## Honest limits of the shop design
- It is a **design only**: nothing is charged, no field holds a value, filter chips and the size or quantity controls do not work in the prototype.
- Products, prices, sizes and product names are **samples, to be confirmed with Sam**. Only the services and prices Sam gave are real facts.
- A working shop needs development (product data, cart, payment provider, delivery rules, tax). That is outside this Figma design.
